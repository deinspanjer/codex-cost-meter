# Proposal

## Why

The November 18 refresh read current GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol prices and their short/long cutoff from OpenAI's flagship pricing section but offered no changes. Reviewers had to reconstruct that relationship from pages of repeated evidence.

## What Changes

- Onboard a new model from explicitly scoped context-band evidence and labeled rates, as one independently reviewable change per model.
- Show the eligibility decision alongside the evidence that explains it. Keep unrelated coverage gaps visible without burying eligible changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `pricing-evidence-refresh`: extend the capability introduced by merged but unarchived `add-local-pricing-refresh`. Archive that baseline before this additive delta.

## Impact

Current Markdown collection, proposal generation and verification, human refresh output, and user documentation. Reuse the existing external catalog and proposal formats. This does not add promotional pricing rules, change historical pricing policy, or publish a release.
